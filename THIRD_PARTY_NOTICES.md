# Third-party components and assets

- **Three.js and OrbitControls**: vendored in `web/public/vendor/`; the MIT license is preserved as `three-LICENSE.txt`.
- **NVIDIA/SimReady models**: downloaded separately. Source URLs and pinned revisions are recorded in `config/warehouse_assets_requested.json` and `docs/WAREHOUSE_MODELS.md`. Their upstream terms apply; they are not relicensed by this repository.
- **AgiBot A3 Ultra assets**: downloaded separately from the pinned Hugging Face dataset documented in `docs/WAREHOUSE_MODELS.md`; consult the upstream dataset terms.
- **Isaac Sim, ROS 2 and the APF workspace**: external dependencies, not bundled here.

No license for the project's original code has been selected yet. Public visibility alone does not grant a general reuse license. Third-party license files remain applicable to their respective components.
